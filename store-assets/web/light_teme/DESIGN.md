## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, sensory refinement, and meticulous craft of high-end culinary arts and artisanal pantries. It marries the timeless elegance of organic parchment and botanical kitchen notebooks with the clarity of modern gastronomic interfaces. The interface feels natural, sunlit, and tactile—evoking heritage kitchens, farm-to-table menus, and curated spice ateliers.

Targeted at discerning home cooks, chefs, and culinary curators, the interface delivers quiet luxury, warmth, and effortless structure. The design style balances **warm minimalism** with **tactile editorial framing**, using fine linear divisions, elevated cream surfaces, and luminous amber-teal accents to create an intimate, sensory browsing experience.

## Colors

The palette establishes an earthy, light-filled culinary environment rooted in parchment tones, balanced with deep natural contrasts:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas: `#F7F4EB` (Warm Parchment) serves as the ambient page foundation.
  - Cards, panels, and floating sheets: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Porcelain White) to provide crisp relief and separation.
  - Secondary containers and input backgrounds: `#F0EAE1` (Pressed Oatmeal).
- **Outlines & Dividers:**
  - Structural hairline borders: `#E6DFCE` (Subtle Mortar) for delicate boundary lines.
- **Typography & Content:**
  - Primary headings & titles: `#1E1A12` (Rich Charcoal) for dense, readable hierarchy.
  - Supporting body text: `#3D3528` (Espresso Ash).
  - Metadata, timestamps, and secondary captions: `#5C5343` (Muted Cacao).
- **Accents & Action Signals:**
  - Primary Accent: `#E5B83B` (Golden Amber) for active states, ratings, focused tab lines, and primary callouts.
  - Secondary Accent: `#00A878` (Emerald Teal) reserved for dietary identifiers, pricing pill tags, freshness badges, and positive metrics.

## Typography

The typography relies completely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric yet humanist structure to create a clean, contemporary rhythm reminiscent of modern culinary magazines. 

Headings command attention through tighter letter tracking and deliberate weight (`700` and `600`), grounding recipe titles and category sections with rich authority. Body levels prioritize open readability for recipe instructions, ingredient measures, and flavor profiles. Small labels, metadata keys, and dietary pills lean on slightly tracked, uppercase or semi-bold weights for rapid glancing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system across desktop, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile:
- **Desktop:** 12 columns, 1.5rem gutters, and 3rem outer page margins (capped at 1320px maximum inner content width).
- **Tablet:** 8 columns, 1.5rem gutters, and 2rem margins.
- **Mobile:** 4 columns, 1rem gutters, and 1rem margins.

Generous vertical breathing space surrounds card clusters and recipe steps to avoid visual fatigue, allowing white container cards to lift cleanly off the parchment background.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, relying on tonal contrast and gentle, warm ambient diffusion:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat `#F7F4EB`. Holds the overall page framework.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, List Rows):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a crisp 1px solid `#E6DFCE` hairline border. Supported by an ultra-subtle warm ambient diffusion (`box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 26, 18, 0.04)`).
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Flyouts, Popovers):** `#FFFFFF` surface with an elevated warm shadow (`box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(30, 26, 18, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 26, 18, 0.04)`). Border softens to `rgba(230, 223, 206, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays):** Deep modal sheet floating over an ambient backdrop dim of `#1E1A12` at 35% opacity with subtle backdrop blur (4px).

## Shapes

The design system employs a **pill-shaped (level 3)** aesthetic for all interactive controls, badges, and floating tags, balanced by generous corner radiuses on structural panels:
- **Buttons, badges, dietary pills, and search inputs:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px).
- **Cards, modular recipe panels, and modal containers:** Large architectural radius (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem to 2rem).
- **Inner elements (thumbnails, media blocks, input textareas):** Intermediate rounding (`rounded-lg` / 1rem).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill):** Solid `#E5B83B` fill, `#1E1A12` text, fully rounded (`rounded-full`). Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover introduces subtle brightness shift with light amber glow.
- **Secondary / Dietary Action:** Solid `#00A878` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, fully rounded. Used for direct purchasing, add-to-recipe, or active dietary filtering.
- **Ghost / Neutral Button:** Transparent fill, 1.5px border in `#E6DFCE`, `#1E1A12` text. Hover shifts background to `#F0EAE1`.

### Badges & Dietary/Pricing Pills
- **Dietary Pills (Vegan, GF, Organic, Spice Level):** Fully rounded pill badge. Soft emerald background (`#E6F6F1`), dark teal text (`#00A878`), font size `label-sm`, weight `700`, uppercase tracking.
- **Pricing & Value Pills:** Solid `#00A878` badge with pure `#FFFFFF` bold numeral text for prices, weights, or batch years.
- **Spice & Origin Tags:** Soft amber background (`#FDF8EB`), rich amber-brown text (`#997314`).

### Cards & Panels
- **Recipe & Spice Cards:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E6DFCE`, rounded `1.5rem`. Inner padding `1.5rem`. Image containers within cards adopt a `1rem` radius. Titles in `#1E1A12`, metadata captions in `#5C5343`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Search & Filters:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full`) bar with `#FFFFFF` background, 1.5px `#E6DFCE` border, and text `#1E1A12`. Focus state transitions the border to `#E5B83B` with a subtle `0 0 0 3px rgba(229, 184, 59, 0.2)` ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circle-centric shape. Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` with `#E6DFCE` rim. Checked: `#00A878` background with pure white icon check/dot.

### Lists & Recipe Step Guides
- Clean horizontal rows divided by 1px solid `#E6DFCE`. Step index markers styled as small circular badges with `#F0EAE1` background and `#1E1A12` bold numbering.
